\## Introduction



Forensic ancestry inference is increasingly performed using ancestry informative markers (AIMs) and high-throughput sequencing technologies.



This study evaluates the robustness of ancestry prediction methods under simulated SNP degradation.



\## Materials and Methods

The reference dataset included 2,504 individuals from the 1000 Genomes Project Phase 3, grouped into five continental superpopulations: AFR (n=661), AMR (n=347), EAS (n=504), EUR (n=503), and SAS (n=489).
